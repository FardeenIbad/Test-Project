# ServQuik Insurance Marketing Website - Proposal

## Executive Summary

This proposal outlines a conversion-focused marketing website for ServQuik targeting independent insurance brokers and agencies (1-10 person operations primarily). The site will center around two flagship offers with proven ROI calculations and risk-reversal guarantees.

---

## Key Insights from Knowledge Base

### Company Positioning
- **Mission**: Automate routine customer interactions so businesses can focus on high-value work without adding headcount
- **Primary ICP**: Independent insurance agencies/brokerages (CA/US) with 1-10 staff experiencing missed calls, slow first response, and desire for more quote opportunities
- **Core Value Prop**: 24/7 AI "employee" that captures every lead across voice, chat, email, and SMS

### Strategic Assets (Social Proof)
- **Buchanan Insurance**: Germania-referred broker who paid $5K for custom AI build handling 21+ product quote flows
- **Germania Insurance Partnership**: ServQuik presenting at 2026 summit; Germania actively referring brokers
- **Bake Sale Toronto**: Multi-vertical proof (non-insurance success story)

### Primary Offers (From Power Offers Database)

**Offer #1: No-Risk 90-Day AI Pilot™ – "Prove It or We Work for Free"**
- Target: 1-8 person agencies, skeptical about AI
- Setup: $0 (waived) / Monthly: $497 for 3 months
- Guarantee: 15+ quote opportunities in 90 days or we work for free
- Scarcity: Limited to 10 agencies

**Offer #2: After-Hours Revenue Rescue™ – AI Receptionist**
- Target: 2-10 person agencies losing after-hours revenue
- Setup: $1,500 / Monthly: $199
- ROI: ~11x in year one (3 extra policies/month = $43K premium vs $3.9K cost)
- Scarcity: 5 agencies/month maximum

## Design Direction

### Visual Tone
- **Industry**: Insurance B2B (professional, trustworthy, modern)
- **Not**: Generic SaaS, overly techy, or consumer-focused
- **References**: Think Lemonade Insurance meets HubSpot (clean, clear, ROI-driven)

### Color Palette
```
Primary:   #1E3A8A (Deep Blue - trust, insurance industry standard)
Secondary: #10B981 (Green - growth, ROI, success)
Accent:    #F59E0B (Amber - urgency, missed opportunity)
Neutral:   #F9FAFB (Light gray background)
Text:      #111827 (Near black)
```

### Typography
- **Headlines**: Inter Bold / Outfit Bold (modern, clean, web-safe)
- **Body**: Inter Regular (highly readable, professional)
- **Accent Numbers/Stats**: Tabular figures for ROI calculations

### Component Style
- **Buttons**: Bold CTAs with high contrast, slight shadow for depth
- **Cards**: Subtle borders, light shadow, white background
- **Icons**: Line-style icons (not filled) for professional look
- **Spacing**: Generous whitespace, clear visual hierarchy

### Imagery Strategy
- **Hero**: Abstract visual of clock/phone/24-7 concept (not stock photos of people)
- **Icons**: Custom insurance-themed icons (phone, chat, calendar, shield)
- **Logos**: Germania logo (with permission), ServQuik logo
- **Avoid**: Generic business handshakes, stock office photos

```

- Name (required)

- Phone (required)
```

  - Output: "You're losing $X/year in after-hours premium"
  - CTA below calculator: "Book Audit to Fix This"

## Technical Specifications

### Stack
- **Framework**: Next.js 14 (App Router)
- **Language**: TypeScript
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v3
- **Forms**: React Hook Form + Zod validation
- **Analytics**: Google Analytics 4 (placeholder integration)
- **Scheduling**: Calendly embed (URL placeholder)
- **Hosting**: Vercel (recommended) or similar

### Performance Targets
- **Core Web Vitals**: LCP < 2.5s, FID < 100ms, CLS < 0.1
- **Mobile-First**: Fully responsive (mobile, tablet, desktop)
- **Accessibility**: WCAG 2.1 AA compliant
- **SEO**: Semantic HTML, proper heading hierarchy, alt tags

### Integration Placeholders
- CRM webhook endpoint (for form submissions)
