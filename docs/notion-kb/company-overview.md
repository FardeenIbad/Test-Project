# Overview of ServQuik

---

# Mission

Automate routine customer interactions so businesses can focus on high-value work and close more revenue without adding headcount.

# Positioning

ServQuik is an AI “employee” platform that delivers 24/7 voice, chat, email, and SMS agents. We integrate with the client’s existing stack, deploy quickly, and use vertical playbooks (insurance-first) to recover missed revenue, improve first response, and standardize service across channels.

# Target Markets

- **Primary:** Insurance agencies and brokerages (CA/US) with meaningful inbound volume (phones + forms), compliance needs, and fragmented intake. ICP signals: consistent missed calls/after-hours volume, slow first response, desire to book more qualified appointments without hiring.
- **Secondary (as needed):** Legal services, home-service trades, multi-location franchises, transportation and other service SMBs that share the same intake and follow-up problems.

# Core Problems We Solve

- Missed calls and after-hours leakage
- Slow first response across channels
- Manual triage and inconsistent intake qualification
- Weak follow-ups and low conversion on inquiries
- Friction in scheduling and handoffs to humans
- Channel sprawl (voice, web, email, SMS, socials) without a unified workflow
- Rising staffing costs for routine customer service
- Compliance and messaging governance (eg. opt-outs, auditability)

# One-Liner

ServQuik builds 24/7 AI employees that capture and qualify leads, handle customer service, and book appointments across voice, chat, email, and SMS.

# Short Story / Credibility

Founded as a field-service software project in 2021 and pivoted in 2024, ServQuik now operates as a Canadian AI-automation business focused on insurance. We ship modular, omnichannel agents with done-for-you setup, insurance-specific playbooks, and integrations for telephony, CRM, payments, and social channels. The result is faster first response, recovered missed-call revenue, and standardized service without extra headcount.
